About PPC.

OUR MISSION

"And make disciples of all nations, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you." Matthew 28:20. To take the command of the Lord and start working in India, the mission is trying to establish churches in the rest of the states and neighboring areas, carry out evangelization using modern facilities and technology, find dedicated ministers and engage them in the mission.

PRAY AND ACT

OUR HISTORY

The prayer partners chain was started on January 1, 2003, at the top of Brother Panakal Johnson's shop on Pattambi Road, Kunnamkulam, with the intention of gathering for the beginning of the mission, starting fellowship for prayer and exchanging prayer materials. Soon after, a prayer magazine, Prayer Partners' Chain, was published, and in 2005, a newsletter, Last Trumpet, was published. In the areas of Ainur Colony and Tiruvilliamala Colony, the work was done under the name of Golgotha ​​Mission Field. Starting in 2013, as part of its North Indian operations, the Mission's first mission field was opened in Kaligaon, a village near Jaipur in Orissa's Koraput district. The mission is going forward as church ministry, tract depot ministry and charity activities in 12 states including Kerala.
